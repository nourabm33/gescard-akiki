Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

This policy explains how personal information is handled when you use this website, operated by Gescard Akiki, a property advisor with RichKey Properties, Dubai Hills Business Park, Building 4, 2nd Floor, Dubai, UAE.

What this website collects

The site uses static hosting. The host and providers may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser type when your browser connects to their services.

The form prepares a message using the details you enter. You decide whether to send it from WhatsApp or your email application. The website does not submit those details to an enquiry server.

How your information is used

  • To respond to your enquiry and discuss your property requirements.
  • To share property information relevant to your request.
  • To keep a record of our communication.

RichKey Properties may receive the information needed to handle your enquiry. An enquiry does not subscribe you to marketing.

Analytics choices

Google Analytics and advertising tags are disabled on this version of the site.

Third-party services

WhatsApp

Messages you send via WhatsApp are processed by WhatsApp (Meta Platforms) under its own privacy policy.

YouTube

Public video information and thumbnails come from YouTube. The player loads after you press play, using YouTube’s privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com). Your use is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Instagram and external links

Public Reel thumbnails may load from Meta. Reel players load from Instagram when you select “Load preview”. Links to Instagram, YouTube, Google Maps and other sites open those services, which have their own privacy policies.

Media cache

The site retrieves a public media list from GitHub and keeps a copy of that list in your browser for the current session. The list contains public video and Reel details.

Retention

Enquiry correspondence is handled through Gescard’s business inbox or the channel you choose. The applicable retention depends on the purpose of the correspondence, provider settings and any record-keeping obligations. Contact us if you want to ask about a particular enquiry.

Third-party providers may process data in other countries. For information about an applicable transfer or how long particular data is retained, use the privacy contact below.

Your rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete the personal information held about you, or object to its use, by contacting Gescard.a@richkey.ae.

Contact
