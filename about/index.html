About Gescard

Meet Gescard Akiki.

Senior Property Consultant at RichKey Properties, specialising in off-plan homes in Dubai and Abu Dhabi.

Introduction

Real Estate Is Personal.

Gescard advises buyers on off-plan homes in Dubai and Abu Dhabi. He begins with a conversation about where you want to live or invest, what you can commit to and when you need the home.

He looks closely at the developer, the masterplan and what is already in place around a project. The details that matter often sit outside the floor plan: the coastline, established schools, nearby jobs and the routes people use every day.

The shortlist reflects your priorities. Gescard helps you compare the homes, payment schedules and delivery milestones that fit your brief.

My Approach

A property search shaped around your goals.

  1. 01

    Understanding your brief

    Every enquiry starts with a conversation about your goals, preferred locations, property type, timing and budget, whether you are investing or looking for a home.

  2. 02

    Exploring suitable options

    Your requirements shape the search. Options are shortlisted around what matters to you, rather than presenting everything on the market.

  3. 03

    Direct communication

    You speak with Gescard directly throughout the search, with clear answers to your questions and support as you consider each next step.

My Philosophy

“Real estate is more than choosing a property. It is about understanding the people behind every decision, recognising their priorities and exploring opportunities that match their ambitions.”
