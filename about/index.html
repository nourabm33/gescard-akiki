About Gescard

Meet Gescard Akiki.

A UAE real estate consultant with RichKey Properties, focused on understanding people before properties.

Introduction

Real Estate Is Personal.

Gescard Akiki is a UAE real estate professional working with RichKey Properties. His approach is centred on understanding each client’s individual requirements and helping them explore properties that fit their goals.

Whether you are considering a property investment, an apartment or a villa, the search is tailored to you: your priorities, your preferred communities and the way you want to live or invest in the UAE.

My Approach

A property search shaped around your goals.

  1. 01

    Understanding your brief

    Every enquiry starts with a conversation about your goals, preferred locations, property type, timing and budget, whether you are investing or looking for a home.

  2. 02

    Exploring suitable options

    Your requirements shape the search. Options are shortlisted around what matters to you, rather than presenting everything on the market.

  3. 03

    Direct communication

    You speak with Gescard directly throughout the search, with clear answers to your questions and support as you consider each next step.

My Philosophy
