Off-plan buyer questions

What to ask before choosing a development, reviewing a payment schedule or making a reservation.

What does off-plan mean?

You buy a unit before it is complete, using the developer’s plans, specification and sale documents to understand what is being delivered. Compare the unit plan and promised specification with the wider masterplan and the payment schedule.

How does an off-plan purchase in Dubai usually work?

Start by confirming the project and developer in Dubai REST. For a specific unit, ask for the reservation terms, the draft sale agreement, the project escrow details and the registration process. The exact steps and timing depend on the development and the contract.

Dubai Land Department project status
What should an overseas buyer check first?

Confirm which ownership rights apply to the property and who can buy it. Ask which documents must be signed, whether they can be signed from abroad, and how the instalments will be paid. An independent adviser can review the contract and any cross-border tax implications.

Dubai Land Department buyer FAQs
Why do the developer and location matter?

Look at the developer’s delivered projects and at the exact plot or building in the masterplan. Visit the area where possible. Compare how you would reach work or school today and which roads, amenities and transport links are still planned.

What should I ask about the payment schedule?

Ask for the deposit, instalment dates, amounts due at construction milestones and the balance at handover. Check when separate charges apply, what happens if a deadline is missed, and which payments and conditions affect a later transfer.

How can I check the project, broker and advertisement?

Use Dubai REST or the Dubai Land Department services to look up Dubai projects and brokers. For a Dubai property advertisement, scan its Madmoun QR code when applicable. For an Abu Dhabi listing, use ADREC’s tool to verify its Madhmoun permit.
