        Infrastructure & Connectivity

        The Future of Dubai

        When I compare locations, I look at what is already in place and what is still being built. Both matter when you’re choosing where to live or invest.

        1. Expansion underway

          Al Maktoum International Airport

          Dubai is expanding its airport at Dubai World Central in phases. The new passenger terminal puts long-term air links and logistics capacity at the centre of Dubai South’s growth plans.

          Official source

        2. Open · Masterplan developing

          Expo City Dubai

          The former Expo site is open, with a wider masterplan for homes, workplaces, event venues and public space. For buyers, the mix of uses is a reason to look closely at what is already within walking distance.

          Official source

        3. Operational

          Jebel Ali Port

          DP World’s port and the adjacent Jebel Ali Free Zone form an established employment and logistics hub. They give context to the road links and job centres around Dubai’s southern waterfront.

          Official source
